# Visual assets

Technology and selected research-service logo paths are from Simple Icons v16.36.0: https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons . Their distributed license is included in `../icons/SIMPLE-ICONS-LICENSE.md`. Brand marks identify the linked services and tools; they do not imply endorsement. SciProfiles, SSRN and Frontiers Loop use original generic profile line icons, rather than purported official marks.

The README portrait composition uses Abdessamad Bourkibate's supplied likeness and a violet circular frame inspired by his reference. The portrait PNG and static headers retain alpha transparency. Animated headers use theme-specific backgrounds. Handwriting motion, section lettering and neutral project-table graphics are decorative profile artwork.
