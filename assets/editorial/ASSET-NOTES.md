# Visual assets

Technology and selected research-service logo paths are from Simple Icons v16.36.0: https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons . Their distributed license is included in `../icons/SIMPLE-ICONS-LICENSE.md`. Brand marks identify the linked services and tools; they do not imply endorsement. SciProfiles, SSRN, Frontiers Loop, Preprints.org, OSF, Research Square, TechRxiv and LinkedIn use assets from their official websites or source repositories; the original files and source links are documented in [Research-service marks](../brand-originals/SOURCES.md).

The README portrait composition uses Abdessamad Bourkibate's supplied likeness and a violet circular frame inspired by his reference. The portrait PNG and static headers retain alpha transparency. Animated headers use theme-specific backgrounds. Handwriting motion, section lettering and neutral project-table graphics are decorative profile artwork.

Navigation and table symbols use original line-icon artwork. Navigation badges link to profile sections; publication-format badges identify the type of record and do not represent an editorial assessment or endorsement.
